Fateme (Fatima) Nateghi

Postdoctoral Researcher · Stanford University · PhD, KU Leuven

Postdoc in the HealthRex Lab at Stanford with Dr. Jonathan H. Chen, building LLMs and evaluation frameworks that hold up in real clinical settings.

I'm also a Data Science Lead in the ARISE network, advancing open benchmarks and real-world evaluation of clinical AI systems. Before Stanford, I completed my PhD in Biomedical Sciences at KU Leuven, where I focused on semi-supervised learning for time-to-event prediction in partially labeled clinical data.

Research

NOHARM

First, Do NOHARM: Towards Clinically Safe Large Language Models
D. Wu*, F. Nateghi*, et al. · arXiv 2026 · *co-first

A large-scale benchmark for evaluating the clinical safety of LLMs using real physician-to-physician consultation cases, the foundation of MAST (Medical AI Superintelligence Test).

arxiv how it works MAST suite leaderboard

ARMD-Predict

Multi-Site Machine Learning to Predict Antibiotic Resistance Before Culture Results
F. Nateghi Haredasht*, N. Marshall*, J. H. Chen, et al. · under review 2026 · *co-first

Machine-learning models that estimate antibiotic resistance from routine EHR and microbiology data before culture results return, developed with data from four U.S. academic medical centers.

web app

SAGE

Specialist AI for Guiding Experts
F. Nateghi, J. H. Chen, et al. · ongoing

A retrieval-augmented system that helps primary care providers route specialty consultations and surfaces guideline-aligned recommendations during real clinical workflows.

website code medRxiv 2025 arxiv 2025 AMIA 2025 talk

Bup-Nal Retention

Predicting treatment retention in medication for opioid use disorder
F. Nateghi Haredasht, et al. · JAMIA 2025 · Addiction 2024

Combining structured EHR data with LLM-extracted features from clinical notes to predict 6-month retention in buprenorphine-naloxone therapy, with an open web tool for risk stratification.

web app JAMIA 2025 Addiction 2024 code

Semi-supervised survival analysis

PhD work · KU Leuven
F. Nateghi, C. Vens · Machine Learning 2022 · Scientific Reports 2023

Methods for time-to-event prediction when most labels are censored, improving outcome prediction without requiring fully observed follow-up.

thesis Machine Learning 2022 code
